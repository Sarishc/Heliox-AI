# Heliox Brand

Heliox uses a geometric H monogram formed by two interlocking ribbons. The
construction references a helix and the opposing cost/performance signals that
the product helps teams reconcile.

## Assets

- `assets/brand/concept-02-mark.svg` — standalone source mark
- `assets/brand/concept-02-wordmark.svg` — dark-background lockup
- `assets/brand/heliox-mark-{16,32,64,180,512}.png` — raster exports
- `assets/brand/favicon.ico` — 16/32/64px browser icon

SVG is the source of truth. Raster assets should be regenerated from it rather
than edited directly.

## Color

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Signal | `#8B5CF6` | Primary brand and actions |
| Trace | `#C4B5FD` | Highlights and helix crossover |
| Graphite | `#090A10` | Primary dark canvas |
| White | `#F8FAFC` | Wordmark and high-emphasis text |

## Usage

- Preserve clear space equal to the width of one vertical monogram stem.
- Use the standalone mark at 16–48px and the full lockup above 120px wide.
- On light backgrounds, use Graphite for the wordmark.
- Keep the monogram upright and preserve its original proportions.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, add a container tile, or substitute colors.
- Do not place the mark on visually noisy imagery or low-contrast backgrounds.
