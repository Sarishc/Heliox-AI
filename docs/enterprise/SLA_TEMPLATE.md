# Heliox Service Level Agreement (Template)

## 1. Service Description

Heliox provides GPU cost management, forecasting, and optimization as a hosted SaaS platform.

## 2. Availability

| Tier | Uptime | Monthly Allowance |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| **Standard** | 99.5% | 3.6 hours downtime |
| **Business** | 99.9% | 43 minutes downtime |
| **Enterprise** | 99.95% | 22 minutes downtime |

**Measurement:** Excludes scheduled maintenance (announced 72h ahead).

## 3. Performance

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| API p95 latency | < 200ms |
| API p99 latency | < 500ms |
| Success rate | ≥ 99.5% |

## 4. Support

| Tier | Response Time | Channels |
|------|---------------|----------|
| **Standard** | 24 business hours | Email |
| **Business** | 8 business hours | Email, Slack |
| **Enterprise** | 4 business hours | Email, Slack, Phone |

## 5. Credits

If SLA is not met, Customer may request a service credit:

- **99.0–99.5%:** 10% of monthly fee
- **95.0–99.0%:** 25% of monthly fee
- **< 95.0%:** 50% of monthly fee

Credits are the sole remedy for SLA breaches.

## 6. Exclusions

SLA does not apply to:

- Customer-side issues (network, configuration)
- Force majeure
- Third-party services (AWS, GCP outages)
- Abuse or violation of ToS

## 7. Reporting

Monthly SLA report available upon request within 10 business days of month end.
